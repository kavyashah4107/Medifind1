How MediFind Works

MediFind helps users find medicines available at nearby participating pharmacies.

1️⃣ Search

Enter the name of the medicine you are looking for.

2️⃣ Find

MediFind checks participating pharmacies for the medicine.

3️⃣ Check Availability

View the pharmacy location, distance and medicine availability.

4️⃣ Visit Pharmacy

Visit or contact the pharmacy to confirm availability.
